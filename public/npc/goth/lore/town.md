# The town
The central town has six ordinary houses, one gothic house, one church and a central stone well with a wooden roof. Tall stone walls surround it. Four gates lead outside, connected by streets through the central plaza.

# Death and respawning
After dying, the player respawns inside the church. The church is the death respawn location.

# Starting a game
At the beginning of a game, the player starts in an ordinary house. The gothic house belongs to the player's girlfriend.

# Beyond the walls
Outside town are pillars, vegetation, lava pools and arena targets. The lava is dangerous. Players can explore ramparts and lookouts above the walls.
