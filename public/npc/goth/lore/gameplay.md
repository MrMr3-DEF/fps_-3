# Grappling hook
The player presses R to fire or release the grappling hook. The grappling hook can pull the player toward rooftops and other high surfaces.

# Walking
WASD moves the player around the arena.

# Jumping and power jump
Space jumps. Power jump starts off at the beginning of a game. It turns on automatically the first time the player leaves the castle walls. After that it stays as the player sets it with Ctrl, including after respawning or going back inside. Ctrl toggles power jump.

# Hovering
The player holds Shift while in the air to hover. Hovering consumes hover fuel.

# Weapons and aiming
Left click fires. E cycles weapons. Number keys 1 through 5 select weapons. Right click or C aims.

# Third person camera
P switches between first-person and third-person camera views.

# Talking to the girlfriend
The player approaches the girlfriend in the gothic house, looks at her and presses F to talk. The girlfriend waves when chat opens and moves her hands when replying. Dialogue cannot give quests, move items or change the game world yet.
